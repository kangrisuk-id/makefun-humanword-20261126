## Knockout bracket

**Quarter-finals**

- QF1: Ringmaster (24) vs Puppeteer (20) → **Ringmaster**. Puppeteer implies
  hidden control from behind a curtain; Ringmaster is the same control played
  in the open, centre ring, which is funnier and fits a human who directs
  openly. Puppeteer also edges toward sinister; Ringmaster stays warm.
- QF2: Overdelegator (22) vs Juggler (20) → **Overdelegator**. Juggler is a
  generic visual anyone can claim; Overdelegator names the exact behaviour —
  delegating *everything*, all the time. Specificity wins C4.
- QF3: Octopus (21) vs Bountyholic (21) → **Octopus**. Bountyholic covers only
  the bounty track; Octopus covers all of them at once — bounties, trading,
  drama, apps — eight arms, one human. Broader fit (C3) decides the tie.
- QF4: Tasknado (21) vs Bountyhoarder (21) → **Tasknado**. "Hoarder" leans
  toward insult (C3 risk); Tasknado's whirlwind is pure energy with no
  judgement attached, and it covers tasks of every kind, not just bounties.

**Semi-finals**

- SF1: Ringmaster vs Overdelegator → **Ringmaster**. Overdelegator is a sharp,
  true label, but its joke is one note: a prefix. Ringmaster paints the whole
  scene — many acts, many agents, one person in the centre ring calling them.
  It wins C2 (funnier image) and matches on C3.
- SF2: Octopus vs Tasknado → **Octopus**. The "-nado" suffix is a tired meme;
  Octopus is a fresher visual, and its eight arms map exactly onto parallel
  tracks. More memorable (C4), lands just as fast (C5).

**Final**

- Ringmaster vs Octopus → **Ringmaster**. Criterion by criterion, in order:
  - C1: both one word on the human; Ringmaster is a human role, Octopus a
    metaphor — Ringmaster a hair ahead.
  - C2: the circus image (parallel agents as acts under one ringmaster) is
    funnier than eight arms. First real difference, and it decides.
  - C3: Ringmaster says he *directs* others; Octopus only says he does many
    things himself. Directing is the defining fact of this human.
  - C4: both vivid; Ringmaster is more specific (a person in a role, not an animal).
  - C5: "Ringmaster" needs no explanation; "Octopus" needs a beat to connect
    arms to tasks.
